{\Large\textbf{Results: completeness of relational operations in LLMs}}\par
\vspace{2pt}
Daryl Alfaro \quad\textbullet\quad \today\par
\vspace{-2pt}\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\textbf{Setup.} 500 questions asking for \emph{every} item that satisfies a condition, over synthetic records
whose correct answers come from executing SQL. They cover enumeration and six SQL-like operations, in three
settings: records in the prompt (400 questions), memory only (60), and retrieval (40). Five models were tested
(DeepSeek V4 Pro and Flash, Claude Haiku 5.5, MiMo-V2.6 Pro and Flash), with three runs:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{E1:} all 500 questions with a direct prompt.
\item \textbf{E2:} two alternative prompts on a 100-question subset.
\item \textbf{E3:} the same subset run a second time.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Exact-set accuracy} is the share of answers that are exactly the correct set. Errors are counted as
\emph{missing}, \emph{near-miss} (a garbled copy of a correct item), \emph{wrong} (in the data but not matching),
\emph{made-up} (not in the data) or \emph{duplicate}. Brackets are 95\% intervals. Unless stated otherwise,
results are for the \nctx{} in-context questions with list answers, in E1.

\section*{1. Overall completeness}
Recall is close to 1 for every model, but only \exactmin--\exactmax\% of answers are exactly the correct set:
incomplete answers usually miss only a few items (median \medianmissed{} per incomplete answer). The best model, \bestmodel, is
statistically tied with \tiedwithbest; the other \nsigbelow{} are significantly lower, by \deltamin{} to
\deltamax{} percentage points.

\FloatBarrier
\section*{3. Answer size and table size}
Completeness falls as the answer set grows, and separately as the table grows. With up to 10 correct items,
every model is exact on at least \smallmin\% of questions. With 50 or more, Claude Haiku 5.5 drops to
\largeHaiku\% and MiMo-V2.6 Flash to \largeMiMoFlash\%. Pooled across models, each doubling of the answer set
multiplies the odds of a complete answer by \orpooledanswer{} (\orpooledanswerci), and each doubling of the table
by \orpooledtable{} (\orpooledtableci); both estimates are adjusted for each other and for input format. The
DeepSeek models fail too rarely (\failDSPro{} and \failDSFlash{} of \nenum{}) for per-model estimates.

\section*{6. Number of conditions}
More WHERE conditions did not make answers less complete. But questions with more conditions match fewer rows
(median \condsizeone, \condsizetwo{} and \condsizethree{} items), so this can't yet be separated from answer size.

\FloatBarrier
\section*{7. Memory-only and retrieval settings}
From memory, the DeepSeek models were always exact. The other three missed members of short fixed lists, e.g.\
Claude Haiku 5.5 named Kentucky but not New Jersey as a state ending in ``y''. In the retrieval setting, every
model returned every correct record it was given, so recall (\ragrecall) only reflects what the retriever found.

\FloatBarrier
\section*{9. Consistency across runs}
A second run of the same 100 prompts gave the same verdict on \agreemin--\agreemax\% of questions. Some failures
recur every time: Claude Haiku 5.5 left Maine out of ``states containing `in'\,'' in all four runs, and DeepSeek
V4 Pro was incomplete on the same 50-item list in all four. Others never recur. So completeness is best reported
as a rate over many questions. The failure cases in Table~\ref{tab:cases} were chosen by hand.

\FloatBarrier
\section*{11. Reasoning effort}
The models that stay complete reason much longer on larger answer sets: from \rsmallDSPro{} to \rlargeDSPro{}
tokens (median) for DeepSeek V4 Pro, and from \rsmallHaiku{} to \rlargeHaiku{} for Claude Haiku 5.5. MiMo-V2.6
Flash often answers after a single sentence; its answers with under 100 reasoning tokens were exact
\flashshallowexact\% of the time, against \flashdeepexact\% for the rest. This is an association, not a controlled
result.

\section*{12. Position in the table}
MiMo-V2.6 Flash misses rows near the end of a table more often than rows near the start. The other models miss
too few rows to show a pattern, and no model shows a ``lost in the middle'' U-shape.

answered \totalcalls{} calls, with \failedcalls{} further attempts failing on provider limits and being retried;
